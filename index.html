About the project

A study hero who also knows how to play

Objective

To use creative expression while learning vocabulary about studying and leisure activities.

Description

Our group created a comic strip about a study superhero. His study habits and free time activities are unique. The story shows his study routine, how he balances studying with free time, and how his hobbies help him succeed.

Skills developed

  • Vocabulary acquisition
  • Artistic expression and creativity
  • Sequencing and storytelling
  • Collaboration and negotiation within groups

Meet the hero

Bát Hùng Vương

In Vietnamese, bát means eight. He has eight study powers, and each power matches one member of our team. He studies hard in class and at home. After that, he goes out to play.

Role
First-year student hero
Motto
Study smart. Play well. Never quit.
Favorite places
The library and the basketball court
Weakness
He can never say no to one more basketball game.

Daily routine

A day with Bát Hùng Vương

Study takes a big part of his day, but free time is part of the plan. Point at a time on the list to see it on the clock.

    Free time activities

    Hobbies that help him study

    He likes

    Playing basketball

    He plays with his friends after homework. It helps him with teamwork and energy. He comes back to his books with a clear head.

    He enjoys

    Listening to music

    He listens to English songs while he rests. It helps him learn new words and relax before bed.

    He loves

    Reading comics

    He reads comics every night. It helps his imagination and his storytelling. That is why this project is a comic strip.

    He often goes

    Cycling

    He rides his bike to school and around the lake. Fresh air wakes up his mind before the first class.
